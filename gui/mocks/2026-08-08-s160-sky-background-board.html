Two things worth knowing before you judge them. First, every option is one opacity or transform on a single layer — the native driver runs it off the JS thread, so none of them costs a frame or re-creates the battery problem one layer down. Second, they are rendered here through a deliberately low-resolution buffer that is then scaled up. That is not a shortcut: it is how soft light is actually made cheaply, and it is what these will look like on the device.

Defaults are set to shipped-subtle. Hit BOOSTED to see the mechanism clearly, then come back to subtle to decide.

My pick, changed by building them bigger: 2 · Aurora. At phone size the single breath from v1 really is too faint to justify itself, and two overlapping fields on non-harmonic periods never repeat visibly — the field is always slightly different without ever showing you a shape. 5 · Grain is the one to look at if Aurora reads as too soft: it makes the black feel like a material rather than an absence, which is the most terminal-native idea here, and it is the cheapest of the six.
